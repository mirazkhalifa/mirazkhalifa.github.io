# What Is Mobile Device Management (MDM)?

Mobile Device Management (MDM) is a technology that helps organizations manage, monitor, and secure mobile devices through a centralized platform.

## Why Do Businesses Need MDM?

Organizations use smartphones, tablets, and other connected devices for daily operations. Managing these devices individually can become difficult as the number of devices grows.

An MDM solution helps organizations apply policies and manage devices from a central console.

## Key Features of MDM

- **Centralized Management:** Manage enrolled devices from one console.
- **Device Enrollment:** Enroll supported devices using available enrollment methods.
- **Policy Enforcement:** Apply security and usage policies.
- **Application Management:** Deploy and manage business applications.
- **Device Monitoring:** Review device information and compliance status.
- **Remote Management:** Perform supported management actions remotely.

## How Does MDM Work?

An organization enrolls its devices into an MDM platform. Administrators then configure policies, manage applications, and monitor device status through the management console.

The exact features depend on the device platform, enrollment method, and MDM solution.

## Conclusion

Mobile Device Management helps organizations simplify device administration and improve control over their managed devices. Businesses should evaluate their device types, security requirements, and management needs before choosing an MDM solution.
